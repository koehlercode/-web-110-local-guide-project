No Filter Cafe

A Steamworks-inspired local cafe in Garner & Clayton, North Carolina

About No Filter Cafe

No Filter Cafe is a local cafe featuring steamworks-inspired eclectic decor in a calm, family-friendly atmosphere. Their menu offers handcrafted coffees, cocktails, and breakfast and lunch options, including their signature Sidewinder Fries.

What to Try

No Filter Cafe offers a wide variety of food and drinks. Some options include:

  • Coffee, Tea, and Cocktails
  • Belgian waffles and breakfast tacos
  • Avocado Toast
  • Y U Ugly Burger
  • Sandwiches & Salads
  • Muffins, Scones, and Cookies

Plan Your Visit

No Filter Cafe is located at 51-E Technology Drive in Garner, NC, with a second location at 9180 Cleveland Road in Clayton, NC.

The cafe is open Monday through Saturday from 6:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. and Sunday from 7:30 a.m. to 6:30 p.m.
